# Vendor Onboarding Policy

Document ID: PROC-02

Category: Procurement

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy defines the due diligence process required before Solara

Technologies Inc. engages a new vendor.



## Section 2: Due Diligence Requirements

2.1 All new vendors must complete a due diligence questionnaire covering

financial stability, data security practices, and compliance history.



2.2 Vendors that will process Restricted or Confidential data (per PRIV-02)

require an additional privacy risk assessment before onboarding.



2.3 Vendors operating in higher-risk jurisdictions require anti-corruption

due diligence per the Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption Policy (COC-05).



## Section 3: Approval

3.1 New vendor onboarding requires sign-off from Procurement and, for

vendors handling personal data, the Data Protection Officer.



## Section 4: Ongoing Monitoring

4.1 Vendors are re-reviewed annually, or sooner if a significant change in

risk profile occurs (e.g. a reported data breach).
