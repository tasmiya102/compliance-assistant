# Competitive Bidding Policy

Document ID: PROC-04

Category: Procurement

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy requires competitive bidding for larger purchases to ensure

fair pricing and vendor selection.



## Section 2: Bidding Requirements

2.1 Purchases exceeding $50,000 require a minimum of 3 competitive bids

before vendor selection, unless a sole source exception applies under

PROC-06.



2.2 Bid evaluations must be documented, including criteria used and

rationale for the selected vendor.



## Section 3: Evaluation Criteria

3.1 Bids must be evaluated on price, quality, delivery timeline, and vendor

risk profile (per the Vendor Onboarding Policy, PROC-02), not price alone.



## Section 4: Vendor Communication

4.1 All bidders must receive the same information and requirements to

ensure a fair and transparent process.



4.2 Employees involved in bid evaluation may not accept gifts or

entertainment from bidding vendors during the evaluation period, per the

Gifts & Entertainment Policy (COC-04).
