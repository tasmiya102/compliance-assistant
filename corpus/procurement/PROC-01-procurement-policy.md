# Procurement Policy

Document ID: PROC-01

Category: Procurement

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy establishes standards for how Solara Technologies Inc.

acquires goods and services, ensuring value for money, fair vendor

treatment, and appropriate approval controls.



1.2 This policy applies to all employees who initiate, approve, or manage

purchases on behalf of Solara.



## Section 2: General Principles

2.1 All purchases must be made for a legitimate business purpose and

through approved procurement channels.



2.2 Purchases must follow the approval thresholds defined in the Purchase

Order & Approval Policy (PROC-03).



## Section 3: Related Policies

3.1 This policy works alongside the Vendor Onboarding Policy (PROC-02),

Competitive Bidding Policy (PROC-04), and Contract Management Policy

(PROC-05).



## Section 4: Conflicts of Interest

4.1 Employees involved in procurement decisions must disclose any conflict

of interest per the Conflict of Interest Policy (COC-02) before

participating in a vendor selection.
