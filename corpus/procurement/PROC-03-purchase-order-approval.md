# Purchase Order & Approval Policy

Document ID: PROC-03

Category: Procurement

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy defines the approval thresholds required for purchases of

different values.



## Section 2: Approval Thresholds

2.1 Purchases under $1,000 require manager approval only.



2.2 Purchases between $1,000 and $10,000 require Director-level approval.



2.3 Purchases between $10,000 and $50,000 require Vice President approval.



2.4 Purchases exceeding $50,000 require Chief Financial Officer approval

and must follow the Competitive Bidding Policy (PROC-04).



## Section 3: Purchase Order Process

3.1 A purchase order must be issued and approved before any purchase

commitment is made with a vendor.



3.2 Purchases made without a prior approved purchase order may not be

reimbursed and may require additional justification.



## Section 4: Emergency Exceptions

4.1 Emergency purchases may bypass standard approval timing but must follow

the Sole Source & Emergency Procurement Policy (PROC-06) and be documented

retroactively within 5 business days.
