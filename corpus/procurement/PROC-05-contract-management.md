# Contract Management Policy

Document ID: PROC-05

Category: Procurement

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy governs how contracts with vendors are drafted, reviewed,

approved, and stored.



## Section 2: Contract Review

2.1 All vendor contracts must be reviewed by the Legal team before

signature.



2.2 Contracts involving personal data processing must include data

protection terms consistent with the Third-Party Data Sharing Policy

(PRIV-04).



## Section 3: Signing Authority

3.1 Contracts under $50,000 in total value may be signed by a

Director-level manager or above.



3.2 Contracts exceeding $50,000 require signature from a Vice President or

the Chief Financial Officer.



## Section 4: Contract Storage

4.1 All executed contracts must be stored in the central contract

repository within 5 business days of signature.



## Section 5: Renewals & Termination

5.1 Contract owners are responsible for tracking renewal dates and

initiating renewal or termination review at least 60 days before contract

expiration.
