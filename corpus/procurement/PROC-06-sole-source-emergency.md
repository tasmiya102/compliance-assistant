# Sole Source & Emergency Procurement Policy

Document ID: PROC-06

Category: Procurement

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy defines when Solara Technologies Inc. may bypass standard

competitive bidding requirements.



## Section 2: Sole Source Justification

2.1 A sole source purchase (bypassing the 3-bid requirement in PROC-04) is

permitted only when a single vendor is uniquely able to provide the

required goods or service (e.g. proprietary technology, existing system

compatibility).



2.2 Sole source purchases exceeding $50,000 require written justification

approved by the Chief Financial Officer.



## Section 3: Emergency Procurement

3.1 Emergency procurement (e.g. urgent business continuity needs) may

bypass standard approval timelines defined in PROC-03, but must still be

approved retroactively within 5 business days by the appropriate approver

for the purchase amount.



## Section 4: Documentation

4.1 All sole source and emergency purchases must be logged and reviewed

quarterly by the Procurement team to ensure appropriate use of these

exceptions.
