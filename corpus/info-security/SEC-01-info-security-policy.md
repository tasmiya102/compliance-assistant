# Information Security Policy

Document ID: SEC-01

Category: Information Security

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy establishes Solara Technologies Inc.'s framework for

protecting information assets, systems, and infrastructure from

unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.



1.2 This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and any third party

with access to Solara systems.



## Section 2: Core Principles

2.1 Information security is a shared responsibility across all employees,

not solely the Security team.



2.2 Access to systems and data must follow the principle of least

privilege, per the Access Control Policy (SEC-06).



## Section 3: Related Policies

3.1 This policy works alongside the Password & Authentication Policy

(SEC-02), Acceptable Use Policy (SEC-03), and Incident Response Policy

(SEC-05).



## Section 4: Enforcement

4.1 Violations of information security policies may result in disciplinary

action, up to and including termination, and may be reported per the

Whistleblower & Reporting Policy (COC-07) if they involve suspected

misconduct.
