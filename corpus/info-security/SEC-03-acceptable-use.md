# Acceptable Use Policy

Document ID: SEC-03

Category: Information Security

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy defines acceptable and unacceptable use of Solara

Technologies Inc. systems, networks, and devices.



## Section 2: Acceptable Use

2.1 Company systems may be used for business purposes and reasonable

personal use that does not interfere with job duties or violate other

policies.



## Section 3: Prohibited Activities

3.1 Employees may not install unauthorized software on company devices

without IT approval.



3.2 Employees may not use company systems to access, store, or transmit

illegal content, or content that violates the Anti-Harassment &

Non-Discrimination Policy (COC-03).



3.3 Employees may not attempt to bypass security controls, including

firewalls, monitoring tools, or access restrictions.



## Section 4: Monitoring

4.1 Solara reserves the right to monitor use of company systems for

security and compliance purposes, consistent with the Employee Data

Handling Policy (PRIV-05).
