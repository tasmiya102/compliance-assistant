# Remote Work & Device Security Policy

Document ID: SEC-04

Category: Information Security

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy establishes security requirements for employees accessing

Solara Technologies Inc. systems remotely or using company devices outside

the office.



## Section 2: Device Requirements

2.1 Company-issued laptops must have full-disk encryption, endpoint

protection software, and automatic security updates enabled at all times.



2.2 Personal devices used to access company email or systems (BYOD) must be

enrolled in the company's mobile device management (MDM) system.



## Section 3: Network Security

3.1 Employees must use the company-approved VPN when accessing internal

systems from outside the office network.



3.2 Use of public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks for accessing Confidential or

Restricted data (per PRIV-02) is prohibited without VPN protection.



## Section 4: Physical Security

4.1 Company devices must not be left unattended in public places and must

be locked when not in use.



4.2 Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT and Security within 2

hours of discovery, per the Incident Response Policy (SEC-05).
