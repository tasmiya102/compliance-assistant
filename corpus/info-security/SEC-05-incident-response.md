# Incident Response Policy

Document ID: SEC-05

Category: Information Security

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy defines the process for identifying, responding to, and

recovering from information security incidents.



## Section 2: Incident Reporting

2.1 Any suspected security incident (e.g. malware, unauthorized access,

lost device) must be reported to the Security team immediately, and no

later than 2 hours after discovery.



## Section 3: Incident Classification

3.1 Incidents are classified as Low, Medium, High, or Critical severity

based on data sensitivity and business impact.



3.2 Incidents involving Restricted data (per PRIV-02) are automatically

classified as at least High severity and follow the notification

requirements in the Data Breach Response Policy (PRIV-06).



## Section 4: Response Process

4.1 The Security team leads containment and remediation efforts for all

Medium severity incidents and above.



4.2 A post-incident review is completed within 15 business days for High

and Critical severity incidents.



## Section 5: Communication

5.1 Internal and external communications regarding a security incident must

be coordinated through the Security and Legal teams to ensure consistency.
