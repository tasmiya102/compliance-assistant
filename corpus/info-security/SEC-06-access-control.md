# Access Control Policy

Document ID: SEC-06

Category: Information Security

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy governs how access to Solara Technologies Inc. systems and

data is granted, reviewed, and revoked.



## Section 2: Principle of Least Privilege

2.1 Employees are granted the minimum level of system access required to

perform their job duties.



2.2 Access to Confidential and Restricted data (per PRIV-02) requires

manager approval and, where applicable, Data Protection Officer sign-off.



## Section 3: Access Provisioning

3.1 New employee access is provisioned within 2 business days of the

employee's start date, based on their role.



## Section 4: Access Review

4.1 System access is reviewed quarterly by managers to confirm continued

business need.



## Section 5: Access Revocation

5.1 All system access must be revoked within 4 hours of an employee's

termination or resignation becoming effective.
