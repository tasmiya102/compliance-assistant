# Conflict of Interest Policy

Document ID: COC-02

Category: Code of Conduct

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy identifies situations where an employee's personal

interests could conflict with the interests of Solara Technologies Inc.,

and establishes a process for disclosure and management of such conflicts.



## Section 2: Examples of Conflicts of Interest

2.1 Having a financial interest (ownership, investment, or compensation) in

a vendor, competitor, or client of Solara.



2.2 Hiring, supervising, or awarding contracts to a family member or close

personal relation without disclosure.



2.3 Using confidential Solara information for personal gain or the benefit

of an outside party.



## Section 3: Disclosure Requirements

3.1 Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest to

their manager and the Compliance team within 10 business days of becoming

aware of it.



3.2 Disclosures are reviewed by the Compliance team, which may require the

employee to recuse themselves from related decisions.



## Section 4: Ongoing Obligations

4.1 All employees complete an annual Conflict of Interest disclosure form,

regardless of whether they believe a conflict exists.



4.2 Failure to disclose a known conflict of interest is treated as a

violation of the Code of Business Conduct (COC-01).
