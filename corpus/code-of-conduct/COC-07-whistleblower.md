# Whistleblower & Reporting Policy

Document ID: COC-07

Category: Code of Conduct

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy provides a confidential channel for employees to report

suspected violations of law, policy, or ethical standards without fear of

retaliation.



## Section 2: How to Report

2.1 Reports may be submitted via the confidential Ethics Hotline, by email

to compliance@solara-tech.example, or directly to a manager or HR

representative.



2.2 Anonymous reports are accepted and will be investigated to the extent

possible given the information provided.



## Section 3: Non-Retaliation

3.1 Solara Technologies Inc. strictly prohibits retaliation against any

employee who makes a good-faith report under this policy.



3.2 Any employee found to have retaliated against a whistleblower will face

disciplinary action up to and including termination.



## Section 4: Investigation Process

4.1 All reports are reviewed by the Compliance team within 5 business days

of receipt.



4.2 Employees who report in good faith are protected under this policy even

if the investigation does not substantiate the claim.
