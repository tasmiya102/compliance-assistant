# Gifts & Entertainment Policy

Document ID: COC-04

Category: Code of Conduct

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy establishes clear limits on the giving and receiving of

gifts, meals, and entertainment involving vendors, clients, and other

external parties, in order to prevent conflicts of interest and maintain

Solara Technologies Inc.'s reputation for ethical dealing.



1.2 This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and temporary staff

of Solara Technologies Inc.



## Section 2: Gift Limits

2.1 Employees may not accept gifts exceeding $75 in value from any single

vendor, client, or business partner within a calendar year.



2.2 Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (including gift cards) of any amount are

strictly prohibited and must be declined or returned.



2.3 Gifts exceeding the $75 limit that cannot reasonably be declined (e.g.

unsolicited items received by mail) must be reported to the employee's

manager and logged with the Compliance team within 5 business days.



## Section 3: Entertainment & Meals

3.1 Business meals with vendors or clients are permitted up to $150 per

person, provided a legitimate business purpose is documented.



3.2 Entertainment expenses (e.g. event tickets, outings) exceeding $200 per

person require pre-approval from a Director-level manager or above.



3.3 Employees may not accept entertainment invitations that involve travel

or overnight accommodation paid for by an external party without prior

written approval from the Compliance team.



## Section 4: Giving Gifts to Others

4.1 Employees may give gifts or entertainment to external parties (e.g.

clients) under the same $75 / $150 / $200 thresholds defined above.



4.2 Gifts or entertainment offered to government officials or public sector

employees are subject to stricter limits under the Anti-Bribery & Anti-

Corruption Policy (COC-05) and generally require pre-approval regardless of

value.



## Section 5: Reporting Violations

5.1 Any employee who becomes aware of a violation of this policy must report

it via the Whistleblower & Reporting Policy (COC-07) channels.



5.2 Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action, up

to and including termination.
