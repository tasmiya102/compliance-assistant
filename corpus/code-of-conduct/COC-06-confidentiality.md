# Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure Policy

Document ID: COC-06

Category: Code of Conduct

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy protects Solara Technologies Inc.'s confidential

information, trade secrets, and proprietary data from unauthorized

disclosure.



## Section 2: Confidential Information Defined

2.1 Confidential information includes, but is not limited to: source code,

product roadmaps, financial data, customer lists, and unpublished business

strategies.



2.2 Confidential information must not be shared with any external party

without a signed Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and appropriate approval.



## Section 3: Employee Obligations

3.1 Employees may only access confidential information necessary for their

job duties.



3.2 Confidentiality obligations continue after termination of employment,

as specified in each employee's signed confidentiality agreement.



## Section 4: Data Handling

4.1 Confidential information stored electronically must comply with the

data classification and handling rules in the Data Classification Policy

(PRIV-02).
