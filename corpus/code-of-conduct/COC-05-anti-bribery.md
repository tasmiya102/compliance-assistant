# Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption Policy

Document ID: COC-05

Category: Code of Conduct

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy prohibits bribery and corruption in all forms, in

compliance with applicable anti-corruption laws in every jurisdiction where

Solara operates.



## Section 2: Prohibited Conduct

2.1 Employees may not offer, promise, give, or authorize anything of value

to any person, including government officials, to improperly influence a

business decision.



2.2 Facilitation payments (small payments to expedite routine government

action) are prohibited without exception.



## Section 3: Gifts to Government Officials

3.1 Any gift, meal, or entertainment offered to a government official,

regardless of value, requires pre-approval from the Compliance team before

being offered. This is stricter than the general limits in the Gifts &

Entertainment Policy (COC-04).



## Section 4: Third-Party Due Diligence

4.1 Before engaging agents, consultants, or intermediaries in any

jurisdiction, the Procurement team must complete anti-corruption due

diligence as outlined in the Vendor Onboarding Policy (PROC-02).



## Section 5: Reporting

5.1 Suspected violations must be reported immediately via the Whistleblower

& Reporting Policy (COC-07).
