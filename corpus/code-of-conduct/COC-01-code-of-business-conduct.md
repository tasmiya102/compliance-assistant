# Code of Business Conduct

Document ID: COC-01

Category: Code of Conduct

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This Code establishes the standards of ethical behavior expected of

every employee, contractor, and officer of Solara Technologies Inc.



1.2 All Solara employees are expected to act with honesty, integrity, and

fairness in every business interaction.



## Section 2: Core Principles

2.1 Employees must comply with all applicable laws and regulations in the

jurisdictions where Solara operates.



2.2 Employees must treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and

must not discriminate on the basis of race, gender, religion, disability,

age, or any other legally protected characteristic.



2.3 Employees must avoid actions that could damage Solara's reputation,

including public conduct that reflects poorly on the company.



## Section 3: Relationship to Other Policies

3.1 This Code works alongside more specific policies, including the

Conflict of Interest Policy (COC-02), Anti-Harassment Policy (COC-03), and

Gifts & Entertainment Policy (COC-04).



3.2 Where a specific policy provides more detailed guidance than this Code,

the specific policy takes precedence.



## Section 4: Enforcement

4.1 Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action up to and

including termination of employment.



4.2 All employees complete annual Code of Conduct training and must

acknowledge understanding of this Code upon hire and annually thereafter.
