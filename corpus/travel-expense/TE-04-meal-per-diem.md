# Meal & Per Diem Policy

Document ID: TE-04

Category: Travel & Expense

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy establishes daily meal allowances (per diem) for employees

traveling on approved company business.



## Section 2: Domestic Per Diem Rates

2.1 The standard domestic meal per diem is $75 per day, covering breakfast,

lunch, and dinner.



2.2 For designated high-cost cities, the domestic meal per diem is $100 per

day.



## Section 3: International Per Diem Rates

3.1 The standard international meal per diem is $90 per day, or the

locally appropriate equivalent for high-cost cities as published by the

Finance team.



## Section 4: Client Meals

4.1 Meals with clients or vendors are reimbursed separately under the

Gifts & Entertainment Policy (COC-04) and are not counted against the

per diem allowance.



## Section 5: Receipts

5.1 Itemized receipts are required for any single meal exceeding $25, per

the Expense Reporting & Reimbursement Policy (TE-06).
