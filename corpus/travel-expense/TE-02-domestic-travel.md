# Domestic Travel Booking Policy

Document ID: TE-02

Category: Travel & Expense

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy sets standards for booking domestic (within-country) travel

for Solara Technologies Inc. business purposes.



## Section 2: Air Travel

2.1 Domestic flights must be booked in Economy class for flights under 5

hours.



2.2 Flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance where possible to

secure lower fares.



2.3 Employees may retain frequent flyer miles or loyalty points earned

during business travel for personal use.



## Section 3: Hotel Accommodation

3.1 Hotel bookings are capped at $250 per night for standard domestic

travel, unless traveling to a designated high-cost city where the cap is

$350 per night.



3.2 Hotel bookings exceeding these caps require pre-approval from a

Director-level manager or above.



## Section 4: Booking Channel

4.1 All travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel portal

to ensure duty-of-care tracking and negotiated rates.
