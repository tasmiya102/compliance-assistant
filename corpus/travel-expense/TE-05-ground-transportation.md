# Ground Transportation & Mileage Policy

Document ID: TE-05

Category: Travel & Expense

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy governs reimbursement for ground transportation, including

rideshare, taxi, rental car, and personal vehicle use for business travel.



## Section 2: Rideshare & Taxi

2.1 Rideshare and taxi expenses for business travel are reimbursable with a

digital or printed receipt.



## Section 3: Rental Cars

3.1 Rental cars must be booked in the "midsize" category or below, unless a

larger vehicle is required for equipment transport, which requires manager

approval.



3.2 Optional insurance add-ons are not reimbursable where the company's

corporate insurance already provides coverage.



## Section 4: Personal Vehicle Mileage

4.1 Employees using a personal vehicle for business travel are reimbursed

at $0.67 per mile, consistent with the standard company mileage rate.



4.2 Mileage claims must include the business purpose, origin, and

destination of the trip.



## Section 5: Parking & Tolls

5.1 Parking and toll expenses incurred during business travel are

reimbursable with receipts where available.
