# International Travel Policy

Document ID: TE-03

Category: Travel & Expense

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy sets standards for international business travel, including

additional approvals and safety requirements beyond domestic travel.



## Section 2: Approval Requirements

2.1 International travel requires pre-approval from a Director-level

manager or above, submitted at least 21 days before departure where

possible.



2.2 Travel to countries on the company's restricted travel list requires

additional approval from the Security team.



## Section 3: Air Travel

3.1 International flights under 8 hours must be booked in Economy class.



3.2 International flights of 8 hours or more may be booked in Premium

Economy class.



## Section 4: Hotel Accommodation

4.1 International hotel bookings are capped at $300 per night, or the

locally appropriate equivalent for high-cost cities, subject to Director

approval for exceptions.



## Section 5: Safety & Insurance

5.1 All international travelers are automatically enrolled in the company's

travel insurance and emergency assistance program.



5.2 Employees must register their itinerary with the Security team before

departure.
