# Travel & Expense Policy

Document ID: TE-01

Category: Travel & Expense

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy establishes guidelines for business travel and expense

reimbursement to ensure responsible use of company funds while supporting

employees' business travel needs.



1.2 This policy applies to all employees traveling on approved company

business.



## Section 2: General Principles

2.1 All business travel must be pre-approved by the employee's manager

before booking.



2.2 Employees should select the most cost-effective reasonable travel

option available, balancing cost against time and comfort.



2.3 Personal travel combined with business travel is permitted but any

additional costs are the employee's responsibility.



## Section 3: Related Policies

3.1 This policy works alongside the Domestic Travel Booking Policy (TE-02),

International Travel Policy (TE-03), Meal & Per Diem Policy (TE-04), and

Expense Reporting & Reimbursement Policy (TE-06).



## Section 4: Non-Compliance

4.1 Expenses that do not comply with this policy may be denied

reimbursement or may require additional manager approval before payment.
