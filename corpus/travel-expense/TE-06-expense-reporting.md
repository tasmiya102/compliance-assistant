# Expense Reporting & Reimbursement Policy

Document ID: TE-06

Category: Travel & Expense

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy defines how employees submit and get reimbursed for

approved business expenses.



## Section 2: Submission Requirements

2.1 Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being

incurred.



2.2 Itemized receipts are required for all expenses of $25 or more.



2.3 Expenses submitted more than 90 days after being incurred will not be

reimbursed except in exceptional circumstances approved by Finance.



## Section 3: Approval Workflow

3.1 Expense reports are routed to the employee's direct manager for

approval, then to Finance for processing.



3.2 Expense reports exceeding $2,000 in total require additional approval

from a Director-level manager or above.



## Section 4: Reimbursement Timeline

4.1 Approved expense reports are reimbursed within 10 business days of

final approval.



## Section 5: Prohibited Expenses

5.1 Personal entertainment, fines, and expenses unrelated to business

purpose are not reimbursable under this policy.
