# Corporate Card Usage Policy

Document ID: TE-07

Category: Travel & Expense

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy governs the issuance and appropriate use of Solara

Technologies Inc. corporate credit cards.



## Section 2: Eligibility & Issuance

2.1 Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel frequently or incur

regular business expenses, subject to manager approval.



## Section 3: Permitted Use

3.1 Corporate cards may only be used for approved business expenses,

including travel, client meals (per COC-04), and approved office supplies.



3.2 Personal purchases on a corporate card are strictly prohibited, even if

the employee intends to reimburse the company later.



## Section 4: Reconciliation

4.1 Cardholders must reconcile all corporate card transactions in the

expense system within 30 days, per the Expense Reporting & Reimbursement

Policy (TE-06).



## Section 5: Card Suspension

5.1 Repeated late reconciliation or misuse of a corporate card may result

in card suspension and disciplinary action.
