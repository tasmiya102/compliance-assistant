# Data Classification Policy

Document ID: PRIV-02

Category: Data Privacy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy defines classification levels for data handled by Solara

Technologies Inc. so that appropriate protections are applied consistently.



## Section 2: Classification Levels

2.1 \*\*Public\*\* — information approved for public release (e.g. marketing

materials). No special handling required.



2.2 \*\*Internal\*\* — information for internal use only (e.g. internal memos).

Must not be shared externally without approval.



2.3 \*\*Confidential\*\* — sensitive business information (e.g. financial

reports, source code). Access restricted to authorized personnel only.



2.4 \*\*Restricted\*\* — highly sensitive personal or regulated data (e.g.

social security numbers, health records, payment card data). Requires

encryption at rest and in transit, and access logging.



## Section 3: Handling Requirements

3.1 Restricted data must be encrypted using company-approved encryption

standards at all times, whether stored or transmitted.



3.2 Confidential and Restricted data may not be stored on personal devices

or personal cloud storage accounts.



## Section 4: Labeling

4.1 All documents and datasets must be labeled with their classification

level at the time of creation.
