# Data Breach Response Policy

Document ID: PRIV-06

Category: Data Privacy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy defines the process for detecting, reporting, and

responding to data breaches involving Solara Technologies Inc. data.



## Section 2: Detection & Reporting

2.1 Any employee who suspects a data breach must report it to the Security

team immediately, and no later than 2 hours after discovery.



2.2 The Security team must assess and classify the severity of a suspected

breach within 24 hours of the initial report.



## Section 3: Notification Requirements

3.1 For breaches involving Restricted data (per PRIV-02), affected

individuals must be notified within 72 hours of confirmation, where legally

required.



3.2 Regulatory authorities must be notified within the timeframe required

by applicable law, coordinated by the Legal and Compliance teams.



## Section 4: Post-Incident Review

4.1 A post-incident review must be completed within 15 business days of

breach resolution, documenting root cause and remediation steps.
