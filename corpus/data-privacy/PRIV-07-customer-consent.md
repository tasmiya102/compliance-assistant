# Customer Data Access & Consent Policy

Document ID: PRIV-07

Category: Data Privacy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy governs how Solara Technologies Inc. obtains consent for,

and provides customer access to, their personal data.



## Section 2: Consent Requirements

2.1 Explicit consent must be obtained before collecting personal data for

purposes beyond providing the core service (e.g. marketing communications).



2.2 Customers may withdraw consent at any time through their account

settings or by contacting support.



## Section 3: Customer Access Rights

3.1 Customers may request a copy of their personal data held by Solara.

Such requests must be fulfilled within 30 days of verified identity.



3.2 Customers may request correction of inaccurate data or deletion of

their data, subject to the retention rules in the Data Retention & Deletion

Policy (PRIV-03) and any applicable legal holds.



## Section 4: Verification

4.1 Before fulfilling an access, correction, or deletion request, Solara

must verify the requester's identity using company-approved verification

procedures.
