# Third-Party Data Sharing Policy

Document ID: PRIV-04

Category: Data Privacy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy governs how Solara Technologies Inc. shares personal data

with vendors, partners, and other third parties.



## Section 2: Requirements Before Sharing

2.1 Personal data may only be shared with a third party after a signed Data

Processing Agreement (DPA) is in place.



2.2 The Procurement team must complete a privacy risk assessment for any

vendor that will process Restricted or Confidential data, per the Vendor

Onboarding Policy (PROC-02).



## Section 3: International Transfers

3.1 Transfers of personal data outside the country of origin require

approval from the Data Protection Officer and appropriate legal safeguards

(e.g. standard contractual clauses).



## Section 4: Ongoing Vendor Oversight

4.1 Vendors with access to personal data are reviewed annually to confirm

continued compliance with this policy and the applicable DPA.



4.2 Any vendor data breach involving Solara data must be reported to Solara

within 48 hours of the vendor becoming aware, per the Data Breach Response

Policy (PRIV-06).
