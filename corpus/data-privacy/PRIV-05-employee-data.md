# Employee Data Handling Policy

Document ID: PRIV-05

Category: Data Privacy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy governs how Solara Technologies Inc. collects, uses, and

protects personal data belonging to its own employees.



## Section 2: Data Collected

2.1 Solara collects employee data including contact information, payroll

details, performance records, and, where legally permitted, background

check results.



## Section 3: Access Controls

3.1 Employee personal data is accessible only to HR staff, the employee's

direct manager (for relevant records), and Payroll, on a need-to-know basis.



3.2 Employees may request access to their own personnel file by submitting

a request to HR, which must be fulfilled within 15 business days.



## Section 4: Monitoring

4.1 Solara may monitor company-owned devices and accounts for security and

compliance purposes. Employees are notified of monitoring practices upon

hire.



4.2 Monitoring data is classified as Confidential per the Data

Classification Policy (PRIV-02) and retained per PRIV-03.
