# Data Privacy Policy

Document ID: PRIV-01

Category: Data Privacy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy establishes Solara Technologies Inc.'s commitment to

protecting the privacy of personal data belonging to employees, customers,

and business partners.



1.2 This policy applies to all personal data collected, processed, or

stored by Solara, regardless of format or location.



## Section 2: Core Principles

2.1 Personal data must be collected only for specified, legitimate business

purposes and not used beyond those purposes without consent.



2.2 Personal data must be kept accurate, secure, and no longer than

necessary, per the Data Retention & Deletion Policy (PRIV-03).



2.3 Access to personal data is restricted to employees who require it for

their job duties, per the Data Classification Policy (PRIV-02).



## Section 3: Roles & Responsibilities

3.1 The Data Protection Officer (DPO) oversees compliance with this policy

and serves as the primary contact for privacy-related inquiries.



3.2 All employees who handle personal data must complete annual privacy

training.



## Section 4: Related Policies

4.1 This policy works alongside the Data Classification Policy (PRIV-02),

Data Breach Response Policy (PRIV-06), and Customer Data Access & Consent

Policy (PRIV-07).
