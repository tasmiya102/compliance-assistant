# Data Retention & Deletion Policy

Document ID: PRIV-03

Category: Data Privacy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026



## Section 1: Purpose

1.1 This policy specifies how long different categories of data are

retained by Solara Technologies Inc. before secure deletion.



## Section 2: Retention Periods

2.1 Customer account data is retained for 3 years after account closure,

unless a longer period is required by law.



2.2 Employee personnel records are retained for 7 years after termination

of employment.



2.3 Financial and tax-related records are retained for 7 years, per

regulatory requirements.



2.4 Marketing and analytics data is retained for 2 years from collection,

after which it must be anonymized or deleted.



## Section 3: Deletion Process

3.1 Data reaching the end of its retention period must be securely deleted

using company-approved data destruction methods within 30 days.



3.2 Deletion requests from customers (e.g. under applicable privacy law)

must be processed within 30 days of verified request, per the Customer Data

Access & Consent Policy (PRIV-07).



## Section 4: Legal Holds

4.1 Data subject to a legal hold (e.g. pending litigation) must not be

deleted, even if its normal retention period has expired, until the hold is

lifted by the Legal team.
