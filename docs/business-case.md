\# Business Case — Corporate Policy \& Compliance Assistant



\## 1. Problem Statement

Solara Technologies Inc. employees frequently have questions about corporate

policies — code of conduct, data privacy, travel \& expense, procurement, and

information security — but policy documents are long, scattered, and hard to

search manually. Employees often guess at answers or skip compliance checks

entirely, creating risk for the company. This project builds an AI assistant

that answers policy questions accurately, citing the exact source clause, and

clearly states when it does not have enough information to answer safely.



\## 2. Target Users

\- \*\*All employees\*\* — quick answers to everyday questions (e.g. "What's the

&#x20; gift limit?", "Do I need approval for this vendor contract?")

\- \*\*Managers\*\* — checking approval thresholds and procurement rules before

&#x20; signing off on requests

\- \*\*New hires\*\* — learning company policy without needing to read every

&#x20; document in full

\- \*\*HR \& Compliance staff\*\* — a first-line reference tool that reduces

&#x20; repetitive policy questions



\## 3. Corpus Description

The corpus is a synthetic (fabricated) set of Solara Technologies Inc. policy

documents, covering five domains:

1\. Code of Conduct

2\. Data Privacy Policy

3\. Travel \& Expense Policy

4\. Procurement Policy

5\. Information Security Standards



At least 30 documents will be generated across these categories, each broken

into clauses/sections with unique identifiers so answers can cite the exact

source location.



\## 4. Domain Guardrails

\- The assistant answers \*\*only\*\* using retrieved corpus content — it does not

&#x20; use outside/general knowledge to answer policy questions.

\- Every answer includes at least one clause-level citation (document + section).

\- If the corpus does not contain enough information to answer confidently, the

&#x20; assistant \*\*abstains\*\* rather than guessing.

\- The assistant does not provide definitive legal or financial advice — it

&#x20; surfaces the applicable policy and lets the human make the final decision.

\- Out-of-scope questions (unrelated to the five policy domains) are declined.



\## 5. Success Metrics

\- \*\*Retrieval quality\*\*: measured via RAGAS metrics (context precision, context

&#x20; recall, faithfulness, answer relevancy) on a golden evaluation set of 20+

&#x20; questions.

\- \*\*Citation coverage\*\*: 100% of answered questions include at least one valid

&#x20; clause-level citation.

\- \*\*Abstention accuracy\*\*: the assistant correctly abstains on questions the

&#x20; corpus cannot answer, rather than fabricating a response.

\- \*\*Reproducibility\*\*: all quality claims can be regenerated by re-running the

&#x20; committed evaluation script.
